scqwatcha @VERSION@

A free, open-source turntable-style sampler: scratch, tape stop, loops, beat repeater, filter and BPM sync.

This installs:

  • VST3 plugin into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit plugin into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app into /Applications

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or newer.
