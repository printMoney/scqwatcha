Installed

Ableton Live: open Settings → Plug-Ins, make sure "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (and/or Audio Units) is on, then click Rescan. Drag scqwatcha onto a MIDI track.

Logic Pro / GarageBand: it appears under Audio Units → scqwatcha.

Click inside the plugin window, then press H for the list of keyboard shortcuts.

Source code, help and updates: https://github.com/printMoney/scqwatcha
