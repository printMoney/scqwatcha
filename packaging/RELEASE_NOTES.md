## Install

1. Download the **.pkg** and double-click it.
2. macOS says it can't verify the installer, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple developer ID. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to scqwatcha. (macOS 14 or older: right-click the .pkg → **Open** → **Open**.)
3. Click through the installer.
4. Ableton Live: **Settings → Plug-Ins**, turn on the VST3 system folder and/or Audio Units, click **Rescan**, then drag **scqwatcha** onto a MIDI track.

Click inside the plugin and press **H** for all keyboard shortcuts.

## What's in 0.1.0

First public beta: platter playback with scratching, tape stop, play/pause and cue restart, loops and 8 loop pads, beat repeater (1/1 to 1/16), BPM sync with a system BPM knob, keep-pitch mode, XY low-pass filter, keyboard shortcuts for everything (follows your keyboard layout), and three skins (Studio Ink, Liquid Glass, Retro '97).

Checked with pluginval (strictness 5), Apple's auval, 153 automated engine checks, and hands-on testing in the standalone app. It has not been tested inside every DAW yet, so please [report anything odd](https://github.com/printMoney/scqwatcha/issues).
